# Birky: the product (defined 14 Nov 2026)

**One line:** Birky is your money friend from S-Pankki, in WhatsApp. S-mobiili is where you see transactions and move money; Birky is who you talk to, any time, like a friend.

See also [core-idea.md](core-idea.md): "S-Pankki doesn't need another app. It needs a friend in WhatsApp."

## What Birky does
1. **Ask directly:** "what did I spend this month?", "how can I cut it?". Real numbers from S-Pankki's data.
2. **People like you:** your age and salary, only averages of at least 30 customers, never one person. Comparison only to reassure or help a choice, never to judge.
3. **Learn:** "teach me about saving", the 2-minute Learn course on your own numbers.
4. **Voice notes:** talk instead of typing.
5. **Bill photo:** send the picture. "Explain it" → Birky only explains. "OK, let's pay it" → Birky prepares the payment.
6. **Save or invest:** tell Birky your plan; it works out the amount and opens S-mobiili with it filled in. Inside the app, Birky (the animated squirrel) walks you over each button; you confirm with Face ID; Birky celebrates in WhatsApp.
7. **Birky never moves money.** You do, in one step.

## Real vs prototype
- Real: the WhatsApp conversation, S-Pankki's challenge data, the live AI.
- Prototype, labelled: the "S-mobiili" screens ("we had no access to the real app"), Face ID, the payment itself.
