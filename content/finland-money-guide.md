# Finland money guide (for Birky, ages 20-30)

Short, factual notes on money topics a 20-30-year-old in Finland runs into. Checked on 2026-11-14. Each section has its official source. If a figure could not be verified on the official source, it is left out. Rules change: always point the person to the source for their own case.

## emergency-fund
- A buffer is money kept apart for surprises (a broken phone, a dentist bill, a gap between jobs), so you don't need credit for them.
- S-Pankki's guidance: a common rule of thumb is two to three times your monthly net pay; its 2024 example of 2,000 € essential costs a month gives a buffer of about 4,000-6,000 €, enough for at least a couple of months.
- Keep it on a separate account, easy to reach, and build it from regular income; small monthly amounts are a fine start.
Source: https://www.s-pankki.fi/fi/artikkelit/nain-varaudut-yllattaviin-menoihin/ · https://www.s-pankki.fi/fi/artikkelit/Puskurirahasto-on-yksinasuvalle-erityisen-tarkea/
Checked: 2026-11-14

## housing-allowance
- Kela's general housing allowance helps low-income households pay for rental, right-of-occupancy or part-ownership housing.
- It is granted to the whole household: one person applies, and everyone's income and assets count. Couples living together are one household.
- Kela says it can cover up to 70% of housing costs, only up to a municipality-specific maximum for accepted costs, and after a basic deductible.
- For 2026 Kela made no index adjustments to the criteria. Most students get the student housing supplement instead (see students).
Source: https://www.kela.fi/general-housing-allowance · https://www.kela.fi/news/changes-to-kela-benefits-in-2026
Checked: 2026-11-14

## students
- Study grant in higher education: 279.38 € a month if you live independently; study grants are not index-adjusted in 2026.
- Student loan with a government guarantee: up to 850 € a month (since 1 Aug 2024). Usually no repayments or interest payments while you get aid; repayment starts about 1-2 years after the last aid payment.
- Finish your degree within the target time and Kela can pay back part of the loan (student loan compensation).
- Since 1 Aug 2025 most students get the student housing supplement: 80% of rent up to a cap, at most 296 € a month in Helsinki, Espoo, Vantaa and Kauniainen, paid only for study months.
Source: https://www.kela.fi/study-grant-higher-education · https://www.kela.fi/student-loan-higher-education · https://www.kela.fi/financial-aid-for-students-housing-supplement
Checked: 2026-11-14

## asp
- ASP is the state-backed savings scheme for a first home. New law in force since 1 June 2026.
- Anyone aged 15 or over who has never owned a home can start; the old upper age limit (44) is gone.
- Save 50-1,500 € a month, at least 20 monthly deposits, and at least 10% of the home's price.
- Benefits: the bank adds a bonus interest of 2-4% (set in the contract), the state pays part of the ASP loan's interest for the first 10 years, and the loan can get a free state guarantee.
- People who signed before 1 June 2026 may keep saving quarterly.
Source: https://www.valtiokonttori.fi/en/reform-of-asp-legislation/ · https://ym.fi/asp-saastaminen-ensiasunnon-ostajalle
Checked: 2026-11-14

## equity-savings-account
- An equity savings account (osakesäästötili) holds listed shares; you can put in only money, at most 100,000 € in total.
- You can have only one at a time.
- Selling and buying inside the account is not taxed; tax is due only when you withdraw, on the part of the withdrawal that is return, not on the money you put in.
- It cannot hold fund units.
Source: https://vero.fi/henkiloasiakkaat/omaisuus/sijoitukset/osakes%C3%A4%C3%A4st%C3%B6tili/
Checked: 2026-11-14

## capital-income-tax
- Investment income (interest, dividends, capital gains, rent) is usually taxed as capital income.
- Rate: 30% on capital income up to 30,000 € a year, 34% on the part above that.
- Equity savings account returns are taxed at the same rates when you withdraw them.
Source: https://vero.fi/en/individuals/tax-cards-and-tax-returns/income/capital-income/ · https://vero.fi/henkiloasiakkaat/omaisuus/sijoitukset/osakes%C3%A4%C3%A4st%C3%B6tili/
Checked: 2026-11-14

## deposit-guarantee
- Deposits are guaranteed up to 100,000 € per depositor per bank; accounts at separate banks are each covered up to that limit.
- All deposits of private individuals are covered.
- If a bank fails, the Financial Stability Authority pays back within seven working days; you don't need to apply.
- Money from selling your own home is covered in full for a limited time if you plan to buy a new home.
Source: https://rvv.fi/en/deposit-guarantee/
Checked: 2026-11-14

## consumer-credit
- Interest on consumer credit may be at most the reference rate plus 15 percentage points, and never above 20% (contracts from 1 Oct 2023). Other credit costs are capped too, at most 150 € a year.
- The caps do not apply to mortgages secured by property.
- "Pay later" invoices and instalments are credit. Online shops may not show credit or deferred payment as the main or pre-selected way to pay.
- Debt trouble: Takuusäätiö's free Debt Helpline 0800 9 8009 (Mon-Thu 10-14), and free financial and debt counselling at public legal aid offices.
Source: https://www.kkv.fi/en/consumer-affairs/credit-and-payments/credit/price-of-credit/ · https://www.takuusaatio.fi/en/services/advice-on-the-debt-helpline-and-ask-about-money-web-chat/
Checked: 2026-11-14

## s-bank
- S-Pankki is a Finnish deposit bank, part of S Group: it is owned by SOK and the S Group cooperatives.
- It offers an ASP account: 1% deposit interest plus 4% bonus interest when you buy the first home, both tax-free.
